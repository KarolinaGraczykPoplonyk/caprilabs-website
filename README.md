# BinaBalance Support

Welcome to the official support page for BinaBalance, developed by Capri Labs Limited.

## About BinaBalance

BinaBalance is a sensory wellbeing experience designed to help adults pause, notice how they feel about eating in that moment and explore their response to immersive sound.

The experience combines a simple before-and-after check with immersive eating and calming sounds, allowing users to reflect on how their response may vary from one listening experience to another.

BinaBalance is designed with a focus on simplicity, self-awareness and responsible use.

## How to use BinaBalance

1. Rate how much you feel like eating something at that moment.
2. Consider what may be driving your urge to snack.
3. Choose an immersive eating or calming sound.
4. Select a session duration and listen using headphones at a comfortable volume.
5. After the session, think back to how you felt before and rate that feeling again.
6. View your session reflection, progress and patterns over time.

There is no “right” result. Your rating may decrease, stay the same or increase. The purpose is to notice your individual response over time.

## Important Information

BinaBalance is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

BinaBalance is a general wellbeing and self-awareness tool. It is not a weight-loss or appetite-suppression tool and should not be used to avoid meals, restrict food intake or ignore genuine physical hunger.

A change in your rating after a BinaBalance session does not show whether your body needs food and should not be used to make decisions about physical hunger.

BinaBalance is not intended to diagnose, treat or manage medical or mental health conditions and is not a substitute for professional medical, psychological or nutritional advice, diagnosis or treatment.

## Privacy

BinaBalance is designed with privacy and data minimisation in mind.

BinaBalance does not require you to create an account or provide your name or email address to complete a session.
