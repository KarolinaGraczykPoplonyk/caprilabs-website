BinaBalance

Exploring immersive sound as a new approach to overeating and stress-related eating management.

BinaBalance is an adult wellbeing app that uses immersive eating and calming sound experiences during moments associated with cravings, stress-related eating, boredom and habitual snacking.

The concept is inspired by research into sensory processing, attention, habituation and eating behaviour, exploring how sound and sensory experience may influence the way eating-related experiences are perceived.

BinaBalance is a general wellbeing tool for adults aged 18+. Individual responses may vary. It is not medical or nutritional treatment and is not intended to replace meals or suppress physical hunger.
