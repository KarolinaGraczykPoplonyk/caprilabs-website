# BinaBalance Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 30 September 2026**

BinaBalance is developed by Capri Labs Limited. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the BinaBalance mobile application.

## Information and Data Collection

BinaBalance does not require you to create an account or provide your name or email address to complete a session.

BinaBalance asks for your date of birth to confirm that you are aged 18 or over. Your date of birth is used for the age check and is not stored by BinaBalance. The app may store locally on your device that age verification has been successfully completed so that you do not need to enter your date of birth each time you use the app.

Session information, including before-and-after ratings, selected triggers, sounds, session choices and session history, may be stored locally on your device so that BinaBalance can provide features such as session history, progress and personal insights.

BinaBalance does not currently use advertising or third-party analytics services.

## Local Data

Information used for BinaBalance session history, progress, personal insights, age-verification status and app preferences is stored locally on your device.

BinaBalance does not currently transmit this locally stored session information to Capri Labs Limited or use it to create a user profile on external servers.

Removing BinaBalance may remove locally stored app data, depending on your device and backup settings.

## Age Requirement

BinaBalance is intended for adults aged 18 years and over.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if BinaBalance's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was last updated.
