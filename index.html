BinaBalance

A sensory wellbeing experience.

BinaBalance is an adult wellbeing app built around immersive listening experiences and personal reflection.

It is designed for moments when urges to snack may be connected with cravings, stress, emotion, boredom or habitual behaviour. Users can explore eating sounds and calming sounds, compare their own before-and-after ratings and observe personal patterns over time.

The concept draws inspiration from research exploring sensory processing, food-related cues, attention and the relationship between sound and perception. BinaBalance does not claim that sound suppresses hunger or treats overeating.

BinaBalance is a general wellbeing and self-awareness tool for adults aged 18+. It is not medical or nutritional treatment and is not intended to replace meals or suppress physical hunger.
