## Current Product

### BinaBalance

BinaBalance is the first digital product developed by Capri Labs.

It is an immersive sound-based wellbeing experience for adults, designed around personal reflection and awareness of eating urges.

The concept draws inspiration from research exploring sensory processing, food-related cues, attention and the relationship between sound and perception.

BinaBalance is a general wellbeing and self-awareness tool for adults aged 18+. It is not medical or nutritional treatment and is not intended to replace meals or suppress physical hunger.
